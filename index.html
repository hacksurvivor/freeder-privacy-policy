Freeder Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

Freeder is an ebook reader for importing local book files and browsing OPDS catalogs.

Data stored on your device

Freeder stores your library, reading progress, bookmarks, notes, settings, downloaded books, and imported book files locally on your device.

Purchases

Freeder Pro is sold as a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchase processing and premium entitlement status are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. Freeder uses this purchase information only to unlock premium features.

Network access

Freeder connects to OPDS catalog URLs when you browse catalogs or download books. Those catalog providers may receive standard network information such as your IP address and request metadata. Freeder does not control third-party catalog providers.

Cloud sync

Cloud sync is not included in the current release. Freeder does not upload your imported books to Freeder servers.

Account creation

The current release does not require a Freeder account.

Contact

For support, use the email address listed on the Freeder Google Play store listing.
